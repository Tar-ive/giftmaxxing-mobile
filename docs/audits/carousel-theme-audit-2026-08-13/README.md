## Why multi-axis typing

A carousel is not one category. `editorial-teacher-gifts`, for example, is a
teacher recipient, back-to-school/thank-you occasion, planning/wellness
interest, mixed budget and shopping intent. Keeping those values separate:

- avoids duplicating a guide under hand-written category names;
- supports recipient × occasion pills generated by the API;
- lets the recommender use interests without exposing generic departments;
- permits validation in both Swift and TypeScript.

`proposed-types.swift` and `proposed-types.ts` define the proposed boundary.
They are documentation only and do not change production code.

## GABRIEL-inspired workflow

The user's reference is identifiable: OpenAI's **GABRIEL** toolkit turns
unstructured text and images into quantitative measurements. Its published
methods include classification, structured extraction, taxonomy bucketing and
a sample-view UI. Applied here:

1. `extract`: title, recipient, occasion, interests, price and intent.
2. `classify`: apply the controlled enums in the proposed types.
3. `bucket`: propose new themes only from currently unmapped evidence.
4. Human review: inspect tiled source slides plus evidence and confidence.
5. Validate: reject unknown enum values, missing evidence and duplicate IDs.
6. Publish a versioned map; never let model output directly alter production.

Primary sources: [OpenAI overview](https://openai.com/index/scaling-social-science-research/)
and [GPT as a measurement tool](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/7517a586-5bfa-4b87-bd3d-6ea0e9e844c7/GPT-as-a-measurement-tool.pdf).

The requested **Back to School — Him** cannot be honestly generated from the
current 30 carousels. Current evidence supports Back to School — Her and Back
to School — Teacher. Source a male student/campus guide before enabling the Him
pill. Other material gaps: baby/young child, grandparents, coworkers/boss,
wedding, new parents and experience-first gifts.

## Proposed validation gates

- Exactly one `themeId` and one `intent` per carousel.
- At least one recipient, occasion, interest and evidence string.
- `confidence=low` cannot publish without manual approval.
- `imageCount >= 2`; video covers and one-frame exports cannot publish as carousels.
- Generated filter labels must correspond to explicit recipient/occasion
  evidence; no inferred gender.
- Catalog and map versions are promoted together.
