# TikTok carousel rules

- Keep titles and subtitles lowercase. Never use all-caps emphasis.
- Use short, relevant copy. One idea per slide; subtitles are optional.
- Use a slim sans-serif treatment with only enough outline or shadow for contrast.
- Do not add Giftmaxxing branding unless explicitly requested.
- Enter hashtags individually and select each TikTok suggestion so it becomes active.
- Add a relevant sound that is at least 60 seconds long.
- Verify every image, active hashtag, sound, caption, order, and schedule before submitting.
- Scheduled TikTok posts are final; they cannot be edited without deleting and re-uploading.
