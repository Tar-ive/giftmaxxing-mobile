Stop buying forgettable gifts. Great gift givers follow five rules. One: solve a micro-friction—replace the leaky bottle, frayed charger, or cold coffee mug. Two: buy the best version of something inexpensive, not the cheapest version of something expensive. Three: turn an inside joke into a physical callback. Four: give guilt-free permission for the hobby, facial, or experience they never buy themselves. Five: keep notes whenever someone mentions a want or annoyance. Don’t ask, “What should I buy?” Ask, “What friction or desire can I solve?”
