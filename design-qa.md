# Design QA — simplified onboarding and Swipe

- Rendered on iPhone 16 Pro simulator at 402 x 874 points.
- Onboarding is two steps: recipient/budget, then positive-swipe leaderboard.
- No onboarding taste-swipe gate, contact permission, invite gate, or coach-mark chain.
- CTA remains visible; recipient cards and leaderboard rows meet 44-point touch sizing.
- Swipe uses one dominant full-height product card, gallery progress, tap navigation, pass/like gestures, and vertical details reveal.
- Product titles and reasons are clamped to prevent overlap with actions.
- Product photos use fill inside a fixed rounded viewport; details use fit so the complete product remains inspectable.
- Empty remote inventory falls back only to the reviewed curated product set.
