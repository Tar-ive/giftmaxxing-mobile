# App Store screenshots

The `6.9-inch/` PNGs are polished 1320×2868 App Store assets composed from
real iPhone 16 Pro Max Simulator captures.

Regenerate after running the screenshot flow:

```sh
swift scripts/compose-appstore-screenshots.swift
```

Source captures stay in the gitignored `build/appstore-shots/` directory.

## Current App Store story

The screenshot sequence now explains one compressed gift-giving journey:

1. Start from gift inspiration curated by a human.
2. Turn the idea into verified, buyable products.
3. Open the right store with an honest purchase path.
4. Finish with a matching wrap kit and presentation plan.
5. Keep the cross-store purchase plan in one per-person cart.

Recommended subtitle:

> Inspiration to wrapped gift

Recommended promotional text:

> Start with hand-curated gift inspiration, find the real products inside it,
> build one purchase plan across trusted stores, and finish with a thoughtful
> wrapping guide—all shaped around the person you're gifting.

Recommended description opening:

> Giftmaxxing compresses the scattered work of gift giving into one calm flow.
> Begin with ideas worth saving, turn them into verified products, organize the
> purchase by person, and add the finishing touches without juggling tabs and
> screenshots.
